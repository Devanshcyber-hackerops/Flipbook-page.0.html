Diet Plan

Age-Wise Healthy Diet Plan

Ancient Wisdom • Modern India
Satvik Ahaar promotes a balanced, nutritious and mindful way of eating by combining traditional Indian food habits with modern nutritional science.
Satvik Ahaar – Age-Wise Nutrition Summary
Age Main nutritional priority
  • 1–5 Growth and development
  • 6–12 Learning and strong bones
  • 13–19 Growth and muscle development
  • 20–39 Fitness and energy
  • 40–59 Heart and metabolic health
  • 60+ Healthy ageing and muscle preservation
The following diet plans are designed for different age groups, considering their growth, energy, immunity, bone health and nutritional requirements.

1) Age group: 1–5 years (Early Childhood)
  • Main goal: Physical growth, brain development and immunity.
  • Meal Suggested food
  • Early morning Milk
  • Breakfast Vegetable poha / soft idli
  • Mid-morning Banana / seasonal fruit
  • Lunch Dal-rice, soft vegetables, curd
  • Evening Homemade paneer / fruit
  • Dinner Vegetable khichdi
  • Important nutrients: Calcium, iron, protein, healthy fats and vitamins.

2) Age group: 6–12 years (School Age)
  • Main goal: Strong bones, concentration, learning and physical activity.
  • Meal Suggested food
  • Early morning Milk + soaked almonds
  • Breakfast Vegetable upma / poha
  • Mid-morning Apple / orange
  • Lunch Chapati, dal, rice, vegetables, curd
  • Evening Roasted chana / sprouts
  • Dinner Roti, paneer or dal, vegetables
  • Important nutrients: Protein, calcium, iron, vitamin D and fibre.

3) Age group: 13–19 years (Adolescence)
  • Main goal: Rapid growth, muscle development, hormonal health and brain development.
  • Meal Suggested food
  • Early morning Milk + almonds + walnuts
  • Breakfast Vegetable paratha + curd
  • Mid-morning Seasonal fruits
  • Lunch Roti, rice, dal, paneer, vegetables
  • Evening Sprouts / roasted chana / milk
  • Dinner Roti, mixed vegetables, dal
  • Before sleep Milk (optional)
  • Important nutrients: Protein, iron, calcium, vitamin B12, vitamin D and omega-3 fats.

4) Age group: 20–39 years (Young Adults)
  • Main goal: Maintain energy, fitness, metabolism and overall health.
  • Meal Suggested food
  • Early morning Water + seasonal fruit
  • Breakfast Vegetable oats / poha
  • Mid-morning Nuts and fruit
  • Lunch Roti, dal, rice, salad, curd
  • Evening Buttermilk / roasted chana
  • Dinner Vegetable khichdi / roti and paneer
  • Important nutrients: Protein, fibre, iron, healthy fats and vitamins.

5) Age group: 40–59 years (Middle Adulthood)
  • Main goal: Maintain muscle strength, heart health and healthy body weight.
  • Meal Suggested food
  • Early morning Water + walnuts
  • Breakfast Vegetable dalia / oats
  • Mid-morning Papaya / guava
  • Lunch Multigrain roti, dal, vegetables, salad
  • Evening Buttermilk / roasted makhana
  • Dinner Vegetable soup, roti, dal
  • Important nutrients: Fibre, calcium, protein, potassium and healthy fats.

6) Age group: 60+ years (Senior Citizens)
  • Main goal: Healthy ageing, bone strength, digestion and muscle preservation.
  • Meal Suggested food
  • Early morning Water
  • Mid-morning Soft seasonal fruit
  • Lunch Soft roti, dal, cooked vegetables, curd
  • Evening Milk / roasted makhana
  • Dinner Soft vegetable khichdi / dal soup
  • Important nutrients: Protein, calcium, vitamin D, vitamin B12, fibre and adequate fluids.

7) Satvik Ahaar – Daily Food Principles
  • Food category Examples Benefits
  • Grains Rice, wheat, jowar, bajra Energy
  • Pulses Moong, chana, dal Protein
  • Fruits Banana, apple, papaya Vitamins
  • Vegetables Spinach, carrot, broccoli Minerals
  • Dairy Milk, curd, paneer Calcium and protein
  • Nuts Almonds, walnuts Healthy fats
  • Healthy fats Groundnut, sesame, limited ghee Essential fats


8) The Satvik Ahaar Golden Rules
  • Eat fresh, seasonal and locally available foods.
  • Include fruits and vegetables every day.
  • Prefer whole grains over refined grains.
  • Include protein in every major meal.
  • Drink sufficient water throughout the day.
  • Reduce excess sugar, salt and highly processed foods.
  • Maintain regular meal timings.
  • Combine healthy eating with physical activity and adequate sleep.
Important health note: These are general educational vegetarian meal suggestions, not individually prescribed diets. Food quantities should depend on body size, activity, growth, allergies and medical conditions. Children under two need age-appropriate feeding, and people with diabetes, kidney disease or other medical conditions may need modified plans. A strict sattvic diet should also be checked for nutritional completeness, particularly vitamin B12, iron and omega-3.
