Food Groups

Food groups are categories of foods that share similar nutritional properties. The main food groups include:

  • Fruits and vegetables
  • Grains (cereals)
  • Legumes (pulses)
  • Dairy products
  • Meat, poultry, fish and eggs
  • Nuts and seeds
  • Oils and fats
  • Sweets and desserts
  • Beverages
  • Herbs and spices
