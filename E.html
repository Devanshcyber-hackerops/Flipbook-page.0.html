Star Thali

7 Days • 7 Star Thalis


AStar Thali is a thoughtfully curated meal plan that combines traditional Indian flavors with modern nutritional science. Each day of the week features a unique thali (platter) designed to provide specific health benefits, ensuring a balanced and wholesome diet for overall well-being.

⭐ Monday – Energy Star Thali
Theme: Energy & Strength
- 2 whole-wheat chapatis
- Moong dal
- Palak sabzi
- Steamed rice
- Cucumber salad
- Buttermilk
⭐ Tuesday – Protein Star Thali
Theme: Growth & Muscle Support
- Multigrain roti
- Chana masala
- Paneer bhurji
- Carrot and beetroot salad
- Curd
- Seasonal fruit
⭐ Wednesday – Immunity Star Thali
Theme: Vitamins & Minerals
- Vegetable khichdi
- Pumpkin sabzi
- Amla chutney
- Green salad
- Curd
- Guava
⭐ Thursday – Brain Power Star Thali
Theme: Brain & Concentration
- Jowar roti
- Mixed dal
- Seasonal vegetable sabzi
- Walnut and seed mixture
- Curd
- Orange
⭐ Friday – Digestive Star Thali
Theme: Gut Health & Digestion
- Moong dal khichdi
- Lauki sabzi
- Cucumber salad
- Homemade curd
- Buttermilk
- Papaya
⭐ Saturday – Strength Star Thali
Theme: Strength & Stamina
- Bajra or jowar bhakri
- Pithla
- Matki usal
- Seasonal vegetable
- Rice
- Buttermilk
⭐ Sunday – Royal Wellness Star Thali
Theme: Complete Nutrition & Enjoyment
- Whole-wheat chapati
- Paneer curry
- Dal
- Jeera rice
- Seasonal vegetable
- Fruit salad
- Small serving of homemade kheer
